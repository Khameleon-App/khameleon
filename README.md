# Khameleon

Khameleon is an AI assistant that works alongside you in the apps you already use, on your own computer.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/Khameleon-App/khameleon/releases/latest)**:

- **Windows 10 or 11:** `Khameleon Setup <version>.exe`
- **Mac (Apple chip):** `Khameleon-<version>-arm64.dmg`

Khameleon is all-in-one: everything it needs is inside the download, and your data stays on your computer.

### First-time install notes

This early version isn't signed yet, so your computer may warn you the first time:

- **Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** if macOS says Khameleon can't be opened, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
